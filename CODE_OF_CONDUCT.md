# Code of Conduct

We want Onionskin to be a place where people can ask questions, report problems and suggest ideas without being put down.

## Our standard

- Be kind and patient. Assume the other person is trying to help.
- Stay on the topic of the project. Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Do not harass, insult, threaten or discriminate against anyone.
- Do not share other people's private information.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it plainly.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space run for the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment, or open a private security advisory on the repository and say it is a conduct report.

This text is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
